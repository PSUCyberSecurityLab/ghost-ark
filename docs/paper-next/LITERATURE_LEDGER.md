## Literature conclusion

The literature eliminates four tempting claims: discovery of a canonicalization
class; a consumer-aware canonicalization theory; JSON parser differential
testing; and AI receipt/control-plane architecture. The surviving delta is
smaller: a reproducible **decision-conditioned measurement** in which a
specified receipt path and an author-supplied equality rule are executed against
version-pinned third-party engines, paired with an explicit negative corpus
result.

That delta is suitable for an arXiv research-note draft or an artifact-oriented
workshop submission. It does not presently support a main-track top-tier
security/systems claim.

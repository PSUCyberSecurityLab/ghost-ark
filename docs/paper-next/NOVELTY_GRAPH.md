## Surviving delta

The only plausible delta is narrow:

> A reproducible, decision-conditioned measurement that takes one selected
> receipt identity, an author-supplied equality rule, and version-pinned
> third-party engines; it records selected different outcomes and places an
> explicit 0/64 corpus null beside them.

This is not a theory of consumer-relative soundness. It is not a JSON parser
fuzzer, a prevalence study, a DSSE vulnerability finding, a deployed
receipt-consumer evaluation, or an agent-governance architecture result.

## Top-five novelty tests

### P01 — bounded receipt-semantics measurement

After a reader has seen canonicalization-obligation and parser-differential
work, the remaining item is the specific controlled linkage from one receipt
identity to author-supplied policy-engine outcomes, paired with an ecological
null. Its claim is useful only if all three boundaries remain prominent.

### P03 — kernel-probe instrument

The remaining item is a declared identity-loss oracle. It does not yet have the
external comparison, adoption, or held-out evaluation needed for a tool-paper
claim; it belongs inside P01.

### P02 — constructible, not observed

The remaining item is the decision to make 0/64 central rather than hide it.
That is sound reporting, not enough standalone empirical scope for a prevalence
paper.

### P06 — strict admission result

The remaining item is a fair within-artifact comparison under a shared
generator. Existing I-JSON/JCS and parser-hardening work make it a mitigation
result, not an admission-profile contribution.

### P04 — cross-runtime matrix

The remaining item is the exact receipt-path matrix. Broader parser-differential
work already owns the discovery and ecosystem-scale claims, so this evidence
supports P01 rather than a separate paper.

## Second-cut decision

No candidate has a defensible theory or system delta for a top-tier venue on
the frozen record. P01 is selected only as a compact research note whose
falsifiable empirical statement is bounded to its declared paths, policies,
versions, and corpus frame. A maintained external receipt relying party plus a
second population is required before revisiting that decision.

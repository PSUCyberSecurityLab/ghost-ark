# Reviewer Kill Sheet

An attack below is a reason to narrow or withdraw a claim, not something to
argue away. The selected paper is viable only as a bounded research note.

| Attack | Verdict | Required manuscript treatment | What would change it |
|---|---|---|---|
| “Consumer-aware canonicalization obligations are already known.” | **Correct and decisive.** | Do not claim theory novelty. Cite the antecedent and describe only the bounded measurement linkage. | A nontrivial result not already covered by canonicalization literature. |
| “JSON parser differential testing is already broader than this.” | **Correct.** | Do not claim a new fuzzer or ecosystem census; position E7/E11 as selected arms. | A controlled comparison against prior tools on a held-out corpus. |
| “A one-field policy is an author-made example.” | **Correct.** | E16 is called an author-supplied policy-engine outcome, never a deployed receipt-consumer result. | A maintained, independently authored receipt policy path. |
| “0/64 may mean the event is absent or the slice is too small.” | **Correct.** | Put the 0/64 result and 16-producer limit in the abstract, results, and conclusion; call it a bounded null, not a prevalence estimate. | A preregistered second population with a defensible unit of analysis. |
| “E12 is not evidence that DSSE is flawed.” | **Correct.** | State that DSSE signs raw payload bytes and is only a corpus source in this study. | Do not pursue an attack framing without a real DSSE boundary. |
| “The headline data are ignored local reports, not release-bundled evidence.” | **Correct.** | Tie exact figures to tagged source anchors and retain the raw-evidence limitation. | Inclusion of reproducible raw reports in an appropriately versioned evidence release. |
| “You evaluated your own receipt path.” | **Correct.** | E11's external canonicalization arms are supporting evidence, not independent replication. | An independent implementation reproducing the protocol and measurement plan. |
| “Strict admission is known practice.” | **Correct.** | Compare it to I-JSON/JCS-style constraints; report only the selected within-artifact comparison. | Maintained-format compatibility and usability evidence. |
| “The composition result is configuration theater.” | **Credible.** | Keep E13 as a supporting result and name the non-default codec; omit all-default/deployment rhetoric. | A preregistered default or maintained-configuration case. |
| “Why is this not an agent-governance systems paper?” | **It should not be.** | Keep AI governance as motivation only; do not make an end-to-end system claim. | Integrated agent runtime and independent evaluation. |
| “Why is this top tier?” | **It is not, on frozen evidence.** | Describe the work as a narrow research note or artifact-oriented workshop draft. | The independent ecosystem study plus second population and reproduction. |

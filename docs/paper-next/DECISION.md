# Decision: the paper the frozen evidence supports

## Publication decision

No frozen thesis supports a definitive or top-tier paper. The selected product
is a narrow, reproducible research-note draft:

**One Canonical Identity, Different Policy Outcomes: A Bounded Measurement of
JSON Receipt Semantics**

It may be useful as an arXiv note or artifact-oriented workshop submission once
its evidence gate and manuscript checks pass. It is not positioned as a main
security, systems, or AI-agent result.

## Winner

P01 wins only in the constrained sense that it is the one thesis able to make
the artifact's strongest result and its strongest adverse result coexist:

- selected receipt paths can assign one identity to pairs for which
  author-supplied, version-pinned policy engines return different outcomes; and
- E12 observed **0 selected pathologies in 64 eligible stored intoto payloads
  from 16 producers**.

The second statement blocks a prevalence narrative. The first statement does
not establish real receipt-consumer behavior.

## Claim actually made

For declared JSON pathologies, a selected Ghost-Ark receipt path, and
author-supplied equality policies executed by named, version-pinned third-party
engines, some pairs assigned one receipt identity yield different recorded
policy outcomes. The paper measures that bounded relation and records its
ecological null.

## Claims not made

The paper does not claim a new canonicalization theory, a new JSON testing
method, a DSSE or Rekor flaw, a rate in real traffic, a result about deployed
receipt consumers, a ranking of engines, a generally suitable mitigation,
semantic truth, model behavior, or cloud/deployment behavior.

## Evidence spine

1. E1 and E1-B establish selected collapse and strict-admission behavior on a
   curated census and declared generator.
2. E7 and E11 place selected outcomes across named, independently authored
   canonicalization arms.
3. E16 supplies the controlled policy-outcome consequence: four engines and
   eight split cells under author-supplied policies. The stored host is
   unrecorded.
4. E12 supplies the necessary adverse result: 0/64 eligible sampled payloads
   from 16 producers, with seven detector controls.
5. E13 remains a configuration-bounded placement result: no all-default
   implementation case is recorded.

## Biggest blocker

There is no independently maintained receipt relying party or second population.
The selected policy equality rules are authored for the experiment. That limits
the work to constructibility and controlled consequence, not importance in an
operational ecosystem.

## Experimental decision

Do one experiment before trying to broaden the paper: preregister a case study
in an externally maintained signed structured-data ecosystem with independently
authored, versioned decision paths. Include an explicit population and
producer-level analysis, a strict baseline, discriminating controls, and a null
outcome that can change the conclusion. Do not treat DSSE as the target merely
because it supplied the E12 corpus.

## Final-three adjudication

| Candidate | Why it survives | Why it cannot stand alone now |
|---|---|---|
| P01 bounded measurement | Joins controlled outcome evidence to the ecology null | No external receipt relying party or second population |
| P03 kernel-probe | Reproducible instrument and declared oracle | No adoption or comparative evaluation |
| P02 constructible-not-observed | Makes the negative result scientific rather than hidden | 64 eligible entries / 16 producers do not support incidence |
